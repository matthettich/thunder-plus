# IF-SOLD: licences and third-party rights in Thunder Plus

Date: 17 Nov 2026. Written by a Haiku worker at the owner's request ("anything I'd need to change if I sold this app, which is unlikely"); the lead spot-checked LXR, Link, the SoundFont licence file and the HANDOVER rename checklist against the repo, not every row. Not legal advice.

## Scope and method

- Branches read: `origin/beta` (the web app as shipped; root, `beta/` and `stable/` are three copies of the web app, not compared byte for byte) and `native` (checked out at `c0e85d2`, the native port). Citations: `beta:path:line` means `origin/beta`; `native:path:line` means the checked-out tree. Full paths are used for files outside the repo.
- Upstream copies read: `/home/claude/deps/` and `/home/claude/u31-deps/`. Their HEADs equal the pins in `native:native/core/third_party/PINS.md` for eurorack, stmlib, DaisySP, CloudSeedCore and airwindows.
- pump: `/home/claude/matthettich/pump` is at `e43ee10`. The pinned commit `deb07d3` is in the same clone, and its `LICENSE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `plugins/machine/NOTICE.md` and pffft `LICENSE.txt` are identical to `e43ee10` (checked with `git diff`, no output).
- Nothing below is legal advice. Where a licence text was not in a file I could read, the row says "NOT FOUND: needs checking". Nothing was recalled from memory.
- Action labels: CHANGE (code or content must be removed or altered), ADD NOTICE (a notice or credit must ship), LAWYER (needs a lawyer or rights holder), CHECK (a fact to confirm), NONE.

## Summary

**Would have to change (as the app stands today)**
1. LXR (GPL-3.0-only): the `lxr/` folder (on `origin/beta` in three copies: root, `beta/`, `stable/`) and its compiled `lxr.wasm`. Either remove it, or release that part under GPL-3.0 with source. The two factory songs use LXR engines and would need rendering or dropping. Any native build with default options contains the module (`native:native/CMakeLists.txt:12`).
2. Ableton Link (`beta:link/`, desktop only): stated as GPL-2.0-or-later. A closed build must leave it out or hold Ableton's proprietary licence.
3. The app's own licence: `UNLICENSED` in `beta:electron/package.json:7` and `beta:package.json:5`. A sale needs a licence or EULA written and chosen.
4. Names: the owner's own checklist (`beta:HANDOVER.md:497-508`) says to rename several UI names before selling (group 7).

**Would have to add: notices / attributions** (keep `beta:THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in every build; `beta:electron/copy-app.js:6` already copies it into the desktop app)
- MIT notices: eurorack (Plaits, Rings, Clouds, Elements, Beads, Warps, Marbles, Tides 2, stmlib), DaisySP, Airwindows, CloudSeedCore.
- pffft BSD-style notice (already in `beta:THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md:84-121`).
- miniaudio (public domain or MIT-0) and libremidi (BSD-2 with RtMidi MIT and ModernMidi BSD-2) notices (`beta:io/LICENSES/`).
- FluidR3 SoundFont MIT notice (`beta:sounds/FluidR3Mono_License.md`).
- SIL OFL font texts (`beta:fonts/OFL-*.txt`), kept as they are.
- Electron's licence files (electron-builder adds them; not read here).
- Native only: fast_float's three licence files; stb_vorbis's licence once it is vendored (not in the tree yet).

**Needs a lawyer's or the rights holder's answer**
- The SoundFont (`beta:sounds/FluidR3Mono_GM.sf3`, 23.7 MB). Its own licence file says the licence of this collection "is currently under discussion and may be subject to change before release" and that sample sources "will be provided upon release" (`beta:sounds/FluidR3Mono_License.md:18`, `:6`).
- Trademarks and names in the UI (group 7), including "LXR-02" and "Erica Synths" (GPL-3.0 section 7(e) terms, `beta:lxr/src/firmware/NOTICE.md:38-43`), and the name "Thunder Plus" itself (`beta:HANDOVER.md:508`).
- Whether an in-process Ableton Link under GPL-2 needs the proprietary licence (the owner's note: `beta:HANDOVER.md:522-529`).
- Origin of the 42 KB `rsData` blocks in the factory songs (`beta:sounds/ELECTRO.thunder`).
- Whether the shipped `ShareTechMono-400.woff` is a modified version (its OFL names a Reserved Font Name, `beta:fonts/OFL-ShareTechMono.txt:1`).

**No change**
- The owner's pump engine code: "All rights reserved" (`pump/LICENSE:4-8`, same at the pin). It stays the owner's to sell. pump MACHINE (GPL) is outside the Thunder Plus build (`native:native/core/third_party/PINS.md:70`, `native:native/core/third_party/fetch.sh:37-38`).
- The AKWF wavetables: CC0 (`beta:wavetables/AKWF-LICENSE.md:1`). Nothing is required; the credit stays as a courtesy.
- The native core and the LXR seam: permissive, no LXR code (`native:native/core/include/thunder/lxr_seam.h:3-9`).
